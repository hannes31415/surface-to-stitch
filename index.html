Arc-length parameterization ensures each loop sits an equal distance along the surface profile — not an equal Euclidean distance. Loop n sits at radius Rn such that the arc from 0 to Rn is exactly n/N of the total arc length.

Stitch placement uses the number-theoretic offset φ = π / lcm(j, k), where j and k are the special-stitch counts in consecutive loops. This maximises angular separation and preserves rotational symmetry.
